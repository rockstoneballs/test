Privacy policy

Applies to the Sunnyside Android app (app.sunnyside.news) and this website. Last updated 7 October 2026.

In short: The website has no ads and no tracking. The app is free with ads, shown by Google AdMob, or ad-free with a subscription. You can use the app without an account. If you sign in, we keep only your name, email address and whether you're ad-free. Your saved posts and settings stay on your device. We never sell your data.

What stays on your device

  • Posts you save, posts you've read, and the cached feed.
  • Your settings: theme, and whether and when to show the morning briefing notification.

This is stored only in the app's private storage (or your browser's local storage on the website). We can't see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data, deletes it.

What the app downloads, and from where

To show you the news, the app downloads:

  • The Sunnyside feed, from GitHub Pages (rockstoneballs.github.io), run by GitHub.
  • A few good-news publications' public RSS feeds directly, so the app stays fresh between feed updates.
  • Pictures and videos from the sites that published them (for example news sites, Reddit, Imgur and 9GAG), plus cat photos from The Cat API and dog photos from Dog CEO.

Like any website you visit, these services receive your device's IP address and basic technical information (such as the app or browser version) when content is downloaded. They handle that under their own privacy policies. Sunnyside doesn't send them anything that identifies you. The website uses no cookies or trackers.

When you open a story's full article, it opens in your browser on the publisher's site, which has its own privacy policy.

Ads in the app

The free version of the app shows ads between posts, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google collects your device's advertising ID, IP address (and from it, your approximate location), and information about your device and how you interact with ads. In the UK, EU and some other places, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google handles this data under its own policies: how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We only allow ads rated suitable for general audiences, and we block categories such as gambling, dating and politics. Subscribers don't see ads, and AdMob isn't started for them.

Accounts

You don't need an account to use Sunnyside. If you sign in (with Google, Apple, or a link sent to your email address), we use Google Firebase to manage your account. We store your name and email address as provided by the sign-in method, a user ID, and whether you have an active ad-free subscription. This data is stored in Google's London region. We use it only to keep you signed in and to remember your subscription across devices.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account, or ask us to delete it. Deleting it removes everything above straight away.

Subscriptions

The ad-free subscription is sold and billed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. To confirm your subscription, we receive the purchase's details from Google Play: a purchase token, its status and when it renews or ends. We keep these with your account until you delete it. You can cancel any time in Google Play.

Feedback and reports

If you send feedback or report a post, we receive what you type, the post you're reporting (if any), your app version and Android version, and your email address only if you choose to give it so we can reply. Feedback is delivered either through our form service (Formspree), or, if you're sent to GitHub to finish it, as a public GitHub issue, so please don't include anything private there. We use feedback only to improve Sunnyside, keep it only as long as it's useful for that, and never sell or share it.

Notifications

The morning briefing is scheduled on your device. It's off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings. No push notification service is involved.

Children

Sunnyside is a general news app and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect any information from children.

Your choices

If you have an account, you can delete it and its data in the app, or ask us to. For a copy of what we hold about you, or to have feedback you've sent deleted, send a message through the feedback form with the email address you used. Under UK and EU data protection law you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Use the feedback button in the app or on the website, or open an issue on GitHub.
