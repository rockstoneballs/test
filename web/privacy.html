Privacy policy

Applies to the Sunnyside Android app (app.sunnyside.news) and this website. Last updated 3 October 2026.

In short: Sunnyside has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your saved posts and settings stay on your device. The only things you send us are what you choose to type into the feedback form.

What stays on your device

  • Posts you save, posts you've read, and the cached feed.
  • Your settings: theme, and whether and when to show the morning briefing notification.

This is stored only in the app's private storage (or your browser's local storage on the website). We can't see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data, deletes it.

What the app downloads, and from where

To show you the news, the app downloads:

  • The Sunnyside feed, from GitHub Pages (rockstoneballs.github.io), run by GitHub.
  • A few good-news publications' public RSS feeds directly, so the app stays fresh between feed updates.
  • Pictures and videos from the sites that published them (for example news sites, Reddit, Imgur and 9GAG), plus cat photos from The Cat API and dog photos from Dog CEO.

Like any website you visit, these services receive your device's IP address and basic technical information (such as the app or browser version) when content is downloaded. They handle that under their own privacy policies. Sunnyside does not send them anything that identifies you, and does not use cookies, advertising IDs or device identifiers.

When you open a story's full article, it opens in your browser on the publisher's site, which has its own privacy policy.

Feedback and reports

If you send feedback or report a post, we receive what you type, the post you're reporting (if any), your app version and Android version, and your email address only if you choose to give it so we can reply. Feedback is delivered either through our form service (Formspree), or, if you're sent to GitHub to finish it, as a public GitHub issue, so please don't include anything private there. We use feedback only to improve Sunnyside, keep it only as long as it's useful for that, and never sell or share it.

Notifications

The morning briefing is scheduled on your device. It's off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings. No push notification service is involved.

Children

Sunnyside is a general news app and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect any information from children.

Your choices

Because we don't hold personal data about you, there's nothing to download or delete on our side, except feedback you've sent. To have that deleted, send another message through the feedback form asking us to, including the email address you used.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Use the feedback button in the app or on the website, or open an issue on GitHub.
