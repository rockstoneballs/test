Delete your Sunnyside account

Sunnyside (app.sunnyside.news, by the Sunnyside team) only needs an account to remember an ad-free subscription. You can delete it, and everything stored with it, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Sunnyside and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account & ad-free, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Use the feedback button (💬) on the Sunnyside website. Choose "Something else", write "Delete my account", and give the email address you signed in with. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What's deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address and sign-in details.
  • Your ad-free status and the link between your account and your Google Play subscription.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Google Play keeps its own record of purchases, as their privacy policy describes.

Your subscription

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in the Play Store: Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
