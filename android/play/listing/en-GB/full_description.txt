Sunnyside is a news app that only shows good news. Open it first thing in the morning instead of the usual doom, and start your day knowing the world is full of people doing good things.

WHAT YOU'LL FIND
• Good news from around the world, with a focus on the UK and Ireland: conservation wins, kindness, community heroes, new species, clean-energy records and more.
• Progress on hard problems: new treatments for cancer, Alzheimer's, dementia and Parkinson's, and real progress on climate change and nature.
• AI for good: how AI is helping doctors spot disease, scientists discover new medicines and rangers protect wildlife.
• A cat or dog photo every few stories, plus wholesome memes and cute animals.

READ IT HERE FIRST
Every story opens on Sunnyside with its opening paragraphs, so you get the gist without leaving the app. Tap "Continue reading" for the full article on the original site. Every story is credited to the outlet that published it.

ONLY GOOD NEWS
Stories come from dedicated good-news outlets and from mainstream news that passes a strict positivity filter. Sunnyside leaves out politics, crime, war, disasters, celebrity gossip, sport, business news, clickbait and anything sad. If something slips through, tap ☁️ Downer to hide it and let us know.

MADE TO BE CALM
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking.
• Top stories or Latest, card or compact view, light and dark themes.
• Save stories to read later and share them with friends.
• An optional morning briefing notification at a time you choose.
• New stories arrive every half hour.

Start your morning on the sunny side. ☀️
